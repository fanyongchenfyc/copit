# Cover letter

**To the Editors of *Computer Physics Communications***

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript

> **CoPiT: A conditional position-induced Transformer for aerodynamic field prediction on irregular meshes**

for publication as a Computational Physics paper.

High-fidelity RANS evaluations remain expensive in many-query aerodynamic design. We propose CoPiT, which couples a position-induced Transformer backbone with multi-stage Feature-wise Linear Modulation so that spatial aggregation and operating-condition dependence are treated separately on unstructured meshes. The method is evaluated on AirfRANS (two-dimensional airfoil fields, including scarce-data and extrapolation splits) and on BlendedNet++ (three-dimensional blended-wing-body surface fields), with matched conditioning ablations and a comparison against graph- and transformer-based baselines.

The manuscript, source configurations and figure assets are available at https://github.com/fanyongchenfyc/copit.

We confirm that the work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and that all authors have approved the submission.
